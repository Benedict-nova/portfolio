HTML

My journey with HTML began with a simple realization: every complex website is essentially just a series of nested boxes. The first time I typed <>Hello World into a text editor and saw those bold, black letters appear in a browser, it felt like a rite of passage. There is a unique satisfaction in the syntax. The way an opening tag <> demands a closing tag creates a sense of order. It taught me the importance of structure—that for every action, there must be a resolution.
